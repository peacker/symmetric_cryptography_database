Full website-vs-source verification pass (2026-07-16)
======================================================

Read essentially every locally-available reference PDF against its family's

~79 have no local reference at all, see item 6 above, and were sanity-checked

8. LOKI89/91's REAL PAPER IS MISSING, AND THE AUDIT TOOLING HIDES IT
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   The `loki` family (data/primitive_families.yaml) cites `loki_ref` (Brown,
   Pieprzyk, Seberry, "LOKI — A Cryptographic Primitive for Authentication
   and Secrecy Applications," AUSCRYPT 1990) with no local copy — there
   never was one fetched for this specific paper.

   This wouldn't normally be a problem (it would just show up as
   KNOWN_MISSING like the other ~79 stubs), except scripts/reference_pdfs.py's
   fuzzy filename-matcher currently mis-resolves `loki` to
   references/1998-loki97.pdf — the LOKI97 AES-candidate paper, a much later
   and structurally unrelated design by the same research lineage. Because of
   this false-positive match, `scripts/test_reference_pdf_coverage.py`
   currently believes `loki` already has adequate source coverage and it is
   NOT in KNOWN_MISSING, so the gap is invisible to CI.

   This is a tooling bug, not a data bug — I did not change loki's own
   family entry, since its notes (LOKI89 by Brown/Pieprzyk/Seberry, AUSCRYPT
   1990, GF(2^8)-based S-boxes, 64-bit block/key, 16 rounds, LOKI91 fixing
   weak keys) match well-established, uncontroversial crypto history and are
   not in question. Two independent things would resolve this fully:
     (a) fetch the actual 1990 LOKI89 paper (or the 1991 LOKI91 revision) and
         add it as references/1990-loki.pdf or similar, or
     (b) at minimum, tighten reference_pdfs.py's fuzzy-match logic (or add an
         explicit exclusion) so "loki" no longer silently matches "loki97"'s
         file, and add `loki` to KNOWN_MISSING in the interim so the gap is
         at least visible and tracked like the other ~79.
   I didn't make this tooling change myself since reference_pdfs.py is used
   by the influence-mining workflow generally, not just this one family, and
   a change there deserves its own focused look rather than a one-off patch
   buried in an unrelated audit pass.

SNARK/STARK/MPC/FHE settings), I did a full citation-crawl literature review
(reading two comprehensive surveys cover to cover: Canteaut et al.'s "Report
on the Security of STARK-Friendly Hash Functions" and Niu et al.'s "SoK:
