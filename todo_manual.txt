3. FAMILIES CITED BY NAME BUT NOT MODELED IN THIS DATABASE
   ---------------------------------------------------------
   Several audited ciphers explicitly cite a predecessor that has no family
   entry of its own here. I deliberately did not invent new families mid-audit
   (that's a scope decision, not something I couldn't do). If you want these
   edges to exist, the family needs to be created first, then I can add the
   edge:

   - LBlock (Wu & Zhang, ACNS 2011) — TWINE's own paper explicitly compares
     itself to LBlock at length ("proposed independently of ours, quite
     similar to our proposal... two important differences"). Worth adding as
     its own primitive family; it's a well-known cipher in its own right.

   - Orthros (Banik, Isobe, Liu, Minematsu, Sakamoto, ToSC 2021) — uLBC's
     strongest citation ("Revisiting the permutation technique used in
     Orthros...") has no target family. Orthros is a low-latency PRF, not
     strictly a block cipher, so you'll need to decide which tier/schema
     shape fits it.

   - Turtle cipher (Matt Blaze) — Tortuga's actual core permutation; Tortuga
     itself is already modeled but its true predecessor isn't.

   - scrypt (Percival) — cited by both POMELO and Tortuga as a KDF/memory-hard
     design influence.

   - Mersenne Twister (Matsumoto & Nishimura) — CryptMT3's PRNG core; already
     reflected structurally via CryptMT3's own state_size_bits=19937 but has
     no separate family for a proper cross-family edge.
